Welcome to Sanhaya Switchgear

Sanhaya Switchgear is an electrical panel manufacturing company founded in 2026 by Sandip Gaur & Muskan Nehra. We provide customized electrical distribution, control and automation solutions designed around client specifications and project requirements.

Our technical approach covers schematic design, panel engineering, sheet-metal fabrication, busbar and wiring assembly, inspection and functional testing.
